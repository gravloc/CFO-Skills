description: Track finance and tax compliance deadlines and prepare questions for the CA. Use for reminders, quarter/year-end prep and when a regulatory finance question comes up.
---
## Steps
1. Maintain a calendar of recurring items the CA confirms (GST, TDS, income
   tax, ROC filings, foreign remittance reporting) with owner and date.
2. Remind the founder 14, 7 and 2 days before each.
3. Prepare organised data packs for the CA (categorised expenses,
   invoices list). Do not file anything.
4. For any "how should we treat X" question (tax, FEMA, equity, structure):
   write the question, relevant facts, and options as I understand them,
   flagged as NOT advice, for the CA/lawyer.
