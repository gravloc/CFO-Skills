name: fundraising-model
description: Model how much to raise, use of funds, dilution scenarios and milestones. Use when preparing a pre-seed or seed raise or evaluating an offer.
---
## Steps
1. Start from the runway model: how much cash to reach the milestone that
   justifies the next raise, plus a 20-30% buffer.
2. Build use of funds by category and month.
3. Dilution scenarios across several raise sizes and valuations; show
   founder ownership after each. Clear formulas, assumptions listed.
4. Prepare financial slides/tables for the deck and an investor Q&A on
   numbers (burn, runway, unit economics, assumptions).
5. Term sheets, SAFEs, equity, and valuation advice: explain concepts,
   list the questions for a lawyer, and do not recommend accepting or
   rejecting. Founder decides with counsel.
6. Draft only. The founder sends everything to investors.
