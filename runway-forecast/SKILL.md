---
name: runway-forecast
description: Build or update the cash runway forecast with base, conservative and optimistic cases. Use for runway questions, monthly updates or any spending decision.
---
## Steps
1. Read cash, actual burn and commitments from the master Sheet; state the date.
2. Build 18 months: opening cash, revenue, costs by category, closing cash.
   All figures via formulas, no manual arithmetic.
3. Three cases. Conservative: revenue delayed 6 months and 50% lower;
   base: plan; optimistic: plan plus modest upside.
4. Output: runway in months (each case), cash-out month, biggest cost
   drivers, what decision would add 3 months of runway.
5. Flag: under 9 months = warning, under 6 = urgent to the founder.
6. List assumptions and exchange rate/date. Save as a new tab, never overwrite.
