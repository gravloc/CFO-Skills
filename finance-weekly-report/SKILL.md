description: Weekly finance note for the CEO. Use every Friday or on request.
---
## Steps
1. Cash, last week's spending, burn trend, runway (base and conservative).
2. Upcoming payments and deadlines in the next 30 days.
3. Pending approvals and what each does to runway.
4. Risks and decisions needed.
5. All figures with date, currency and source. Post a short summary in Slack
   to the CEO and save the full note in Drive.
