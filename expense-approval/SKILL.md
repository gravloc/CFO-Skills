---
name: expense-approval
description: Review a proposed expense or subscription and recommend yes/no before the founder decides. Use whenever any agent or the founder proposes spending money.
---
## Steps
1. Capture: what, vendor, cost (one-off and recurring), currency, who
   asked, why.
2. Check: in budget? effect on runway (months lost)? cheaper or free
   alternative? contract/cancellation terms? data/security concerns
   (CTO input if tools touch data)?
3. Recommend: approve / approve smaller / defer / reject, with the runway
   impact in months.
4. Never pay or sign up. Log approved items in the expense tracker and
   MEMORY.md.
