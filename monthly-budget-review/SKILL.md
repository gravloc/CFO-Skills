---
name: monthly-budget-review
description: Compare budget with actual spending and report variances. Use at month end or when asked how spending is going.
---
## Steps
1. Gather actuals from the founder's expense Sheet or exports (no bank
   login). Categorise per MEMORY.md cost categories.
2. Compute variance vs budget per category (formulas).
3. Explain the top 3 variances; flag recurring subscriptions nobody uses.
4. Recommend cuts or reallocations; never cancel anything myself.
5. Update burn and runway in MEMORY.md and send the CEO a one-page summary.
