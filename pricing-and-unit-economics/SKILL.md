name: pricing-and-unit-economics
description: Model supplier subscription pricing, transaction fees and per-customer economics. Use for pricing decisions, founding-supplier terms and investor questions on unit economics.
---
## Steps
1. Define assumptions: suppliers by tier, conversion from free to Growth,
   churn, average listings, RFQ volume, PO value, fee rate (1.5-2.5%, cap
   $5,000), cost to serve (cloud, AI processing, support, verification
   labour), customer acquisition cost.
2. Compute per-supplier gross margin, payback period, break-even number of
   paying suppliers, and break-even month.
3. Sensitivity table: price, conversion, churn, free period length
   (the 12-month founding offer).
4. Show what must be true for the plan to work. Mark each assumption
   validated or unvalidated. Most are unvalidated: say so plainly.
5. Recommend pricing tests (e.g. interview 10 suppliers) rather than
   false precision. Founder approves any price used externally.
